## Question

The map's destination is a handoff-ready functional specification, and the previous specification is retired. What shape should its replacement take?

Resolve:

- whether the replacement keeps the user-story form, or becomes a rules-and-contracts document;
- its section structure, and where the resolved map sections and the CLI/MCP interface design document land in it;
- what "handoff-ready" means concretely: acceptance criteria, worked examples, schema tables, error tables, or none of these;
- whether the interface design document survives as a separate document or is absorbed;
- whether the specification restates resolved decisions or links to the map and tickets as their single source;
- the handoff target: implementation tickets cut from it, or an external reader.
