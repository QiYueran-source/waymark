Resolution: Archive is reversible retirement/hidden state, distinct from closed/completed status. Archived items retain their status and all relations and can be explicitly queried/restored; archiving does not change whether a dependency is satisfied (only its closed semantics do). Parent, hard dependency, soft-link, and custom reference targets remain resolvable after archive. Physical delete is not a normal initial command; if added later it must be explicit and refuse to break any references unless a deliberate repair/migration is performed. Soft links do not block; custom reference validation follows the field schema decision.
