## Parent

[Cross-item write consent, archive safety, and dangling references](wm-62yfmh5j41t5)

## What to build

Archiving an item rewrites that item and nothing else. Other people's dependencies, parents, and soft links that point at it stay exactly where they are, and their presence no longer stops the archive. The result names every item it left untouched and who still references the archived item. Consent cleans up inbound references on my own items only; an item of mine is cleaned, somebody else's is left and named.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Archive without consent changes only the target document; every other document keeps its bytes and `updated_at`
- [ ] An archive succeeds while another owner's item still depends on, soft-links to, or is parented by the target
- [ ] The result lists the untouched documents with the relation that referenced the target, and counts them
- [ ] `archive --cascade` rewrites inbound documents owned by the operator and leaves foreign ones untouched and named
- [ ] `HAS_ACTIVE_CHILDREN` still refuses an item with an unarchived child, consent or not
- [ ] The archived item's own relationship fields are preserved exactly as before
- [ ] `archive --dry-run` reports precisely the documents and leftovers the real run then produces on the same project
